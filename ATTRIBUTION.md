## What was adapted

- The project description and Exhibits 1 to 6 were converted from Word, RTF and PDF files to Markdown (see `docs/source/README.md`).
- Figures from Exhibits 1, 2 and 4 were transcribed into structured scenario data in `scenarios/`.
- The four roles in the project description were redesigned as an interactive web application (see `docs/PLAN.md`).

Material adapted from the source project remains under CC BY 4.0.

## Company filings

Exhibits 1 and 2 excerpt the annual reports (Form 10-K) that Best Buy Co., Inc. and NVIDIA Corporation filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission for their fiscal years ended in January 2021.

Exhibit 4 is based on the Best Buy excerpt, with fiscal 2022 figures invented by the source project's authors for a forensic accounting exercise. Those figures are not Best Buy's reported results.
