**Exhibit 3 **

**MGT155 Course Project**

**Examples**** of financial statement adjustments **include but are not limited to:

- Estimation of accounts receivable.

- Factoring or pledging of accounts receivables.

- Depreciation of long-term assets using straight-line method or double-declining method, as well as using different estimated useful lives.

- Impairment of long-term assets.

- Inventory method choice between weighted-average method, FIFO, and LIFO method.

- Estimation of contingent liability.

- Use sales incentives to increase sales revenue in the short-term.

- Make estimations for sales returns, warranty liabilities, etc.

- Bad debt estimation using allowance method or bed debt write-off method.

- Estimation of amortization of long-lived assets.

- Estimation of impairment of Goodwill.

- Counting operating lease as finance lease, and vice versa.
